## MODIFIED Requirements

### R-RTY-002 — A retryable pre-output failure retries over a byte-identical transcript, and every attempt is visible

When G4 fires, the harness MUST re-invoke `Turn` for the **same logical turn** over a transcript that is **byte-identical** to the one the failed attempt was built from. Byte-identity MUST be **proved by the recorded requests**, not assumed: every request the provider captured for that logical turn MUST compare equal as `ai.Request` values.

Nothing may be written to the transcript between two attempts of one logical turn. In particular the failed attempt MUST leave no partial entry behind, the steering drain MUST NOT run between two attempts of one logical turn (a steered message belongs to the *next* logical turn, `R-RUN-008`), and the harness MUST NOT call `CloseTurn` on a failed attempt.

Each attempt MUST be **visible on the stream as its own turn bracket**: its own `turn_start`/`turn_end` pair, its own freshly minted turn identity, on the one contiguous 1-based lane the run's single shared `LaneStamper` owns (`R-RUN-003`). Attempts are visible, not silent.

**No new `EventKind` is introduced.** A retried attempt is distinguished from a new turn by transcript identity — a retry's request transcript equals the failed attempt's, which is exactly what this requirement already guarantees — not by a labelled event. `CheckStream` MUST accept the recorded multi-attempt stream **unmodified**, with `stream_check.go` byte-unchanged (`agent-run-driver/spec.md:125`).

**Back-annotation (AG-17, 2026-08-19) — this pin is HELD, and AG-17's seam placement is what holds it.** AG-17 inserts a context-strategy seam into `Harness.Run`. Two placements were available, and only one keeps this requirement provable:

- **Inside the attempt loop (rejected).** A seam consulted per attempt is a seam that — once AG-18 gives its verdict compaction teeth — could rewrite the transcript **between two attempts of one logical turn**. That does not merely risk breaking the byte-identity clause above; it defeats the exact argument the implementing comment relies on (`harness.go:518-529`): the attempt loop reuses "the transcript slice built once above ... **BY REFERENCE** across attempts — this, not an assumption, is what makes 'identical transcript' provable". A per-attempt seam makes the *provable* into the *merely intended*.
- **At the turn boundary (shipped).** The seam is a nil-default field on the harness, consulted in the outer per-logical-turn loop between the transcript resolution (`harness.go:498`) and the attempt loop (`harness.go:530`), **exactly once per logical turn** regardless of how many attempts that turn takes. No consultation can therefore occur between two attempts, and this requirement stays true **by construction**.

Two further AG-17 properties are recorded because they are what make the "held" claim checkable rather than asserted. *(1)* The transcript handed to the strategy is a **fresh clone**, so a third-party strategy that rewrites what it received cannot rewrite the harness's slice — the by-reference reuse above is safe even against a caller-written strategy. *(2)* AG-17 registers **no** `EventKind` and emits **no** event on any path, so the per-attempt bracket sequence this requirement enumerates is unchanged, `CheckStream` still accepts the recorded multi-attempt stream unmodified, and `stream_check.go` stays byte-unchanged. **No clause of this requirement is amended by AG-17**; the annotation records the decision that preserved it, and AG-17's own cardinality bite (`S-CTX-003`) is what proves the rejected placement would have been observable.
