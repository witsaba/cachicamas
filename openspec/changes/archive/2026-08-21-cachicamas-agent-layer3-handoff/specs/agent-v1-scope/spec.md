## Why this delta is mandatory even though no test fails

`S-AGS-066`'s enforcing test asserts the absence of wall clocks in the hook file and the inertness of a
zero-value registration value. **It asserts nothing at all about who removes the singular pre-request
field.** So AG-23 can freeze the surface, decline the removal, and leave the sentence *"Its removal is
AG-23's"* standing as a promise nobody can now keep — a pure spec-text falsification, archiving as false
with a green suite saying nothing. That is the same class AG-22's own delta on this requirement existed to
close, and `S-AGS-069` is the reason it does not recur here.

The second half is the same class in the other direction: the harness's forwarder could crash a goroutine
the caller does not own, through the deliberately unrecovered seam this requirement's own posture keeps
open. Freezing a v1 surface while that is true would make the compatibility statement false on the day it
lands.

## MODIFIED Requirements

### R-AGS-016 — AG-20 discharges G11 / R-17 and widens seam 1; the concrete hooks stay Layer 3's and the two MUST NOT be conflated (AG-20 addition, 2026-08-20)

**G11 / R-17 is DISCHARGED for its Layer 2 half, and what discharged it is recorded as a mechanism rather than as a reassurance.** The forward pass maps **R-17 to G11** (`S-AGS-030`), and doc 0003's AG-20 charter closes it. What AG-20 shipped:

- **The full taxonomy behind one registration surface.** Four hook points — pre-request (AG-08's, now composed), pre-compact, post-turn and session-start — registered on **one** exported value type held as a **field** on the harness, transported to the turn loop on the harness's own per-turn copy. A registration **method** was unavailable by fence and none was added.
- **A uniform mutate-versus-observe contract enforced BY TYPE, not by documentation.** The two mutating families take a payload and return one with an error; the two observing families **have no result parameters at all**, so a hook that could signal a mutation or a failure is unconstructible. Every payload is a value type with unexported fields and read-only accessors.
- **The asynchrony discipline made mechanical**, which is the charter's own acceptance clause and the standard `R-AGE-008` sets: a per-run observer lane whose enqueue never blocks, dispatch on the lane's own goroutine asserted as a **goroutine-placement** property, a value-stripped observer context that makes the delegation publishing seam unreachable from a hook, a stalled observer proven to delay no event delivery by stream byte-identity, and **no wall clock anywhere** in production or test.
- **Typed, source-named failure on both mutating points**, attributed by source name rather than by a chain-wide ordinal, so the attribution survives a later insertion.

**SEAM 1 IS WIDENED, NOT RE-OWNED.** `S-AGS-023` maps seam 1 to **AG-08.1**, and that mapping is unchanged. AG-08 shipped the seam as a single callable and recorded, in its own spec, that *"AG-20 widens to chain composition"* (`agent-pre-request-hook/spec.md:19`). AG-20 discharges that promise **additively**: the shipped field is kept, runs **first**, and feeds the chain's element 0. Its removal was assigned to **AG-23** and AG-20 did not take it. **(AG-23 resolution: the removal is DECLINED and the field is FROZEN INTO v1.)** AG-23 is the milestone that freezes the surface a Layer 3 application may consume, and removing an exported member is a breaking change — the milestone that freezes a surface is the worst possible place to take one. The field is therefore kept, unamended in behaviour, carries no deprecation marker, and is published in the compatibility statement as **frozen-and-superseded with a post-v1 removal path**. Because AG-23 is Layer 2's **last** milestone, this assignment MUST NOT be re-forwarded: no requirement may continue to name AG-23 as the holder of the removal (`agent-layer3-handoff`'s `R-L3H-009`).

**What is NOT discharged, and this is the clause most at risk of being wrongly closed.** `S-AGS-048` records *"G11's concrete hooks to CO-24.1 / CO-24.2"* — doc 0004's Layer 3 nodes. **AG-20 ships zero concrete hooks.** Cache-breakpoint placement, compaction policy and telemetry are Layer 3's, verbatim from the charter, and this milestone's own out-of-scope table names them. **A taxonomy is not a wiring.** The G11 row's Layer 3 half stays open with CO-24.1 / CO-24.2 named as its holders, on exactly the AG-15 argument this artifact already records: *a shipped seam with a declining implementation is a held place, not a delivered capability*.

**Two further absences MUST be stated so they are inherited knowingly rather than discovered:**

1. **No wall-clock timeout, deadline or "slow hook" threshold ships, and none ever will in Layer 2.** `R-RUN-010` forbids the temporal answer on a structurally similar seam, and `R-AGS-015` forbids Layer 2 deciding Layer 3's content — a threshold is Layer 3 policy. AG-20's "eventually" is the run's **terminal boundary**, a structural moment, not a duration.
2. **`R-AGE-009`'s multi-consumer fan-out is NOT shipped.** AG-20 makes a *hook* non-blocking; it does not build consumer attachment machinery. Envelope invariant 3 closes jointly with AG-01.1; `R-AGE-009` remains decided-and-unbuilt.

**Wave 5's exit is a scheduling fact, not a scope claim.** AG-20 is Wave 5's last milestone, and AG-21 inherits this milestone's frozen postures by name: the caller-owned stalled-observer goroutine leak, the release-before-baseline test rule, unrecovered mutating hooks, and cross-run hook state.

**Back-annotation (AG-21) — all four inherited postures were HONOURED, and each is recorded with what honouring it actually cost.** Wave 6 opens with AG-21, so the inheritance above is now discharged posture by posture rather than left as a forward statement:

| Inherited posture | How AG-21 honoured it |

A wholesale sweep would have sampled a baseline across AG-20's deliberately-gated observer tests and reported AG-20's by-design behavior as AG-21's leak — one of the four verified grounds on which D-B rejected the literal reading |

**What this back-annotation does NOT claim.** It does **not** close `S-AGS-048`'s G11 Layer 3 half: AG-21 ships zero concrete hooks, exactly as AG-20 did, and closing that row would over-claim on the same AG-15 argument recorded above. It does **not** discharge `R-AGE-009`, which remains decided-and-unbuilt. It does **not** close R-05's invariant 3, already closed by `AG-01.1 + AG-20.2`. And it introduces **no** wall-clock threshold: absence 1 above holds for AG-21 too, whose one legitimate clock is the wind-down bound and is a ceiling, never a synchronization point (`NFR-CNH-002`).

**Back-annotation (AG-22) — the exported surface gains a telemetry SEAM and still declares no telemetry SINK, and the two MUST NOT be conflated either.** AG-22 is the milestone that makes `S-AGS-066`'s *"no telemetry sink"* conjunct require a distinction it did not previously need, so the distinction is drawn here rather than left for a reader to guess at:

- **What AG-22 adds to the surface: one optional, exported OpenTelemetry API tracer-provider field on the harness.** Unset, it defaults to the tracing API's own no-op provider, so a **zero-value harness is inert and byte-identical in behaviour** to one built before AG-22 (`agent-observability-boundary/R-AGO-001`, `R-AGO-009`). It is the tracing instance of the same injection rule Layer 1 already carries (`R-AOB-001`, `R-APC-016`).
- **What a *sink* means, and why AG-22 ships none.** A sink is a provider registration, an exporter, an SDK path, or any read or write of process-global telemetry state. AG-22 registers nothing, exports nothing, reaches no SDK, and consults no global getter or setter — the last is asserted, not assumed (`R-AGO-001`, `S-AGO-001`, `S-AGO-004`). The SDK, the exporters and `otelslog` remain denied below the composition root by `R-AGP-003`'s forbidden-prefix table, which AG-22 does not touch.
- **It is not a hook, and it closes nothing of G11's Layer 3 half.** AG-22 registers **no hook of any family**. It instruments the runtime directly at four seams. The `:318` clause above — *"telemetry are Layer 3's"* — is about a **concrete telemetry hook**, and it stands exactly as written: `S-AGS-048`'s G11 row still names CO-24.1 / CO-24.2 as the holders, and AG-22 does not take them.
- **No wall clock is introduced.** Absence 1 above holds for AG-22 too. Its detached-tool fixtures drive the scheduler's **injected wind-down bound**, which is a ceiling rather than a synchronization point — the same carve-out `S-AGS-067` and `NFR-CNH-002` already record for AG-21 — and AG-22 adds no timeout, deadline, sleep or poll of its own.

**Back-annotation (AG-23) — Layer 2 exits, the surface freezes, and the unrecovered-panic posture is KEPT while ceasing to be able to crash a goroutine the caller does not own.** AG-23 is Layer 2's last milestone, so every forward statement in this requirement either discharges here or has nowhere left to go. Four dispositions, each stated because a reader could otherwise infer the opposite:

- **The exported surface gains and loses NOTHING.** AG-23 ships two new **sibling** packages — an importable test kit and a consumer proof — and adds no exported member to Layer 2, removes none, and changes no signature arity. The event stream stays at its committed kind count with AG-23 registering none. The pre-request field's declined removal is recorded in the SEAM 1 paragraph above.
- **The unrecovered-panic posture STANDS, unweakened.** A panicking mutating hook still propagates **uncontained**: AG-23 recovers nothing, swallows nothing, wraps nothing, and converts no panic into an error or an event. What changes is only what the **harness** leaves behind during that unwind: its per-attempt event forwarder now provably terminates before the consumer sink is closed, so the panic can no longer take out a goroutine the caller does not own (`agent-run-driver`'s `R-RUN-014`). **A crash-class defect reachable through a seam this milestone freezes is a defect, not a limitation** — it is fixed here rather than entered in the known-limitations register, because entering it would make the compatibility statement false on the day it lands.
- **No wall clock, no fan-out, no telemetry sink, no concrete hook, no cross-run state.** Absences 1 and 2 hold for AG-23 too, and it registers no hook of any family. `S-AGS-048`'s G11 row **still** names CO-24.1 / CO-24.2 as the holders of G11's concrete hooks: AG-23 hands Layer 3 the contract for wiring them, which is the opposite of wiring them.
- **The Layer 3 vocabulary discipline stops being an intention.** `R-AGS-015` forbids Layer 2 deciding Layer 3's content; AG-23 makes that mechanical for the first time, by a committed scan over the new trees' code **and prose** for concepts specific to one kind of application (`agent-layer3-handoff`'s `R-L3H-010`).
